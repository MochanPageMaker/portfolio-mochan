ABOUT THE DEMOS

Open. Click around. Explore.

All five demos run in your browser with no installation. Use fictional details: forms demonstrate a flow but do not send real enquiries. No payment is processed.

What works in this public version?

Navigation, responsive pages, filters, carts, demo checkout, charts and CSV export. Cart data and some store scenarios are saved only in your browser. User and server-side data management is available in a separate server version.

Were these made for real brands?

These are concept projects based on client briefs, using fictional brands and data. No conversion or other commercial results are claimed. The store includes six demo products, and the school redesign is compared with an original concept created for this project.
